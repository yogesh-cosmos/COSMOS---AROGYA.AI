What it does

Three ways to make sense of your care, in one app.

Scan & understand

Medicine strips, handwritten prescriptions, and lab reports — photographed and explained: what it's for, dosage, side effects, and what an abnormal value means.

Find the right care

Nearby hospitals and pharmacies from OpenStreetMap, with what's known about their specialties — so you're not guessing which one to go to.

Ask, out loud

A chat & voice assistant for hospital choice, medicine questions, and treatment doubts — running locally, in Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Malayalam or English.
